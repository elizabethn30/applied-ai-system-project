# Model Card: Music Recommender Simulation

# What are the limitations or biases in your system?
The limitations with my system is that the training dataset is still very small. The system also relies on keywords from the user's input, and a lack of keywords that the model knows may cause some issues. 

# Could your AI be misused, and how would you prevent that?
The AI could be misued. I would prevent misuse of the AI by making sure the AI does not give information or make a recommendation based on facts that were not given to them. 

# What surprised you while testing your AI's reliability?
I was surprised that the AI could handle cases that were not specifically hard-coded in. The way the AI handled the integer input was really shocking to me, and I was very impressed that the AI could do so much with little context. 

# Describe your collaboration with AI during this project. Identify one instance when the AI gave a helpful suggestion and one instance where its suggestion was flawed or incorrect.
One instance when the AI gave a helpful suggestion was when it recommended that I include logging in my system architecture design because it would help with error checking and verification. 
One instance where the AI's suggestion was flawed was when it said I should use the google-generativeai library instead of the google-genai library. However, I researched this, and I learned that the AI was trying to get me to use a deprecated library, so I went with the original library and the google-flash-lite model. If I had used the library that the AI wanted me to use, I probably would have been stuck trying to figure out why the model I chose was not working.
